ROCKSTAR MEDIA
Viral & Trending Website — starter package

HOW TO OPEN LOCALLY
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Double-click index.html.
3. The site will open in your browser.

HOW TO ADD ADSTERRA
1. Log in to your Adsterra publisher account.
2. Create the ad unit you want.
3. Copy the Adsterra code.
4. Find the text "Adsterra" in the HTML files.
5. Replace the placeholder text inside the ad-slot with your Adsterra code.
6. Repeat for each placement you want.

IMPORTANT
- Replace the sample article text with your own original content.
- Replace the sample Privacy Policy and Terms with versions appropriate to your business/jurisdiction.
- The contact form is only a visual demo; connect it to your email/backend before publishing.
- Before going live, add your favicon, social links, analytics and real article images.
- Do not click your own ads or encourage visitors to click ads. Follow Adsterra's publisher policies.
